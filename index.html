Laboratory

  • Each Builder Potion adds 1 hour at 10x, so n potions save up to 9 × n hours per builder. They need 10 × n hours left to give the full saving. With less, you save 90% of what remains.
  • Each Research Potion adds 1 hour at 24x, so n potions save up to 23 × n hours. They need 24 × n hours left. With less, you save 23/24 of what remains.
  • Extra potions extend the duration only. The speed stays 10x or 24x. This follows the Clash of Clans wiki, though some sites claim the speed stacks, so check in-game once.
